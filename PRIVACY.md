# Privacy Policy for Track Exploder

**Last updated: 28 July 2026**

Track Exploder is an open-source app that extracts and remixes barbershop
part-left learning tracks. This policy explains what it does with your data.

The short version: **Track Exploder does not collect, transmit, or share any of
your data.** Everything happens on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Track Exploder has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We have no
servers, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent.

## Your audio files

When you open audio files, the app reads them and processes them entirely on
your device to extract and mix the parts. Your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

- Files you open are read only after you select them yourself.
- While a mix is being prepared, the app writes temporary working files to its
  own private cache directory on your device.
- Exported files are saved to the location you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes its cache and settings. Files you exported to your
own storage stay where you put them.

## What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally, using your device's app
storage. It never leaves your device:

- Export presets you choose to save.
- A list of your recent exports, so they can be reopened from within the app.

## Network access

Track Exploder makes no network requests. It does not check for updates, phone
home, or contact any service.

The app does declare the Android `INTERNET` permission, which is added
automatically by the Tauri application framework it is built on. The app does
not use it.

The About screen contains links to the project's source code, its funding page,
and the open-source libraries it uses. If you tap one, your device opens it in
your normal browser, and from that point the site you visit is governed by its
own privacy policy, not this one.

## Permissions

Track Exploder asks for access to files only so that you can pick the tracks you
want to work on and save the mixes you create. It does not access contacts,
location, the camera, the microphone, or your device identifiers.

## Children

Track Exploder is a music practice tool and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address
and the date above will be revised. Because the app collects nothing, we do not
expect substantive changes.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app generally, can be raised at
<https://github.com/Safrone/track-exploder/issues>.
